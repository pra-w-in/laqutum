# LaquTum
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Aptitude learners preparing for placements and examinations, as evidenced by the existing content and workflows. User confirmed equal priority for phones and laptops.

## Product Purpose
Teach aptitude through explanations, worked examples, practice questions, hints and mistake review.

## Capabilities and Constraints
User explicitly requires all existing features and content to remain: authentication, saved progress, diagnostic, topic library, adaptive practice, study plan, mistake notebook, exam guide, AI tutor with image input, study groups and notes, challenges and QR joining, paid topic access and account controls. Preserve current backend contracts, entitlements, Supabase services and content bank. Existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript stack.

## Brand Commitments
Keep LaquTum name. User rejects the current design and requests a completely new interface, easier to use and without generic AI aesthetics. No user browser automation; local isolated rendering is permitted.

## Evidence on Hand
content-bank.json and existing frontend modules. No invented testimonials, performance claims, users or exam partnerships.

## Product Principles
Prioritize learning over promotion. Make current task and recovery clear. Keep features discoverable on both phone and laptop. Preserve real account and progress data.
